Dezaemon 2 Parity Mapデザエモン2

What the importer already decodes of a Sega Saturn Dezaemon 2 save, and what still stands between it and full feature parity. Target is the Saturn release (big-endian SH-2, BackUpRam Format BUP saves, LZSS-compressed 8-section payloads) — not the SNES original, which has its own map, nor the two PlayStation Dezaemons, which have theirs. The canonical RE notes live in packages/shmup-engine/FORMAT.md; the pipeline imports and, since 2026-09-05, exports — exportLevelToSav writes a MiSTer-layout cart that boots and plays in Mednafen; real hardware is still untested.

.sav → bup-parse → decompress (LZSS) → decode sec0–3 CG · sec4 palettes · sec5 stages · sec6 BGM · settings → mapSaveToGame → editor game.json → Phaser runtime
Implemented container · graphics · levels · enemies · bosses · items · weapons · BGM · 3D decode Partial traced but not yet wired: MAIN geometries, bullet steering Absent endings

01What remains

Two reverse-engineering blitzes (2026-08-28) closed most of the original twelve items, a third (2026-09-01) closed the title screen and the staff roll, and an adversarial pass (2026-09-12) closed three more and turned up two items that were not on the list. TRACED means the layout is read out of the engine and written down in FORMAT.md and only the runtime is missing; MEANING OPEN means the bytes are located but their semantics are untraced; NO TRACE means nothing exists yet.

  1. 1

    Write path — .sav exportDONE 2026-09-05

    Landed: an LZSS compressor that mirrors the decoder's ring (src/compress.js, within ~2% of the Saturn's own stream sizes), the section-table/checksum builder and BUP block allocator (src/bup-write.js — header at block 2, 0xFF-interleaved 32 KB + 512 KB image, 1,114,112 bytes), and the game assembler (src/write/game-to-save.js: CG pages under a Saturn or Super Famicom palette target, placement grids, enemy records, boss trailers, sprite banks, settings, songs). Exposed as exportLevelToSav, deno task build:sav and the editor's DOWNLOAD .SAV / → SAVE SHELF rows. Verified by re-import through this very parser and the editor (tests/sav_export_test.ts), and by booting, loading and playing the exported foo cart in Mednafen 1.29 with the real BIOS (2026-09-05); real hardware and MiSTer still untested. Still unwritten: credit strips, real item-icon art, 3D models.

  2. 2

    MAIN-weapon geometry for types 2–6TRACED 2026-09-12

    The weapon system was already exact — burst caps [0,3,3,1,1,6,3,2] and reload intervals [0,6,5,12,1,3,6,28], with the power-scaled drain correctly confined to type 7. The five geometries that were still fired straight ahead are now read leaf by leaf out of the handlers at +0xCA7C / +0xDF20 / +0xD06C / +0xE214 / +0xE664: muzzle offsets, per-level shot counts, damage tables and the class each shot spawns. What that trace also settled is that every per-level table in both weapon dispatchers is indexed by the OPTION count (0x0608411C, ship byte +2 bits0-2, capped at 4), not the power level (0x06084120, byte +1). FORMAT.md called it power throughout, and the corpus proves it cannot be: initial OPTION never exceeds 4 across all 262 saves while initial POWER reaches 7, and the tables have five entries. The runtime has not been rewired to any of this yet.

  3. 3

    sec7 part-shape meshes (ポリ吉 part library)TRACED

    Mostly closed 2026-09-02. The meshes were never in the overlay: the disc's MDLDT_01–56.CMP are the editor's part library, 56 files × 4 SGL meshes, each with three colour tables, and the shape word decodes as family:3 | colourSet:2 | meshIndex:7 (decode-model.js · decode-mdldt.js). A model viewer (/editor/model-viewer.html, Phaser 4 Mesh2D) draws every composition from the shipped mesh-library.json; DAIOH's ship renders as its own ポリ吉-made sprite. All three conventions were then traced through the overlay's SH-2 code (FORMAT.md · sec7 shape word): the family → file order is the resolver's own arithmetic (pages 0–5 = files 01–08 / 09–26 / 27–35 / 36–44 / 45–53 / 54–56), parts compose as T·Rx·Ry·Rz·S (SGL post-multiplies slRotX, slRotY, slRotZ), and the per-model colour word is a whole-model tint the shader folds into every polygon colour. The shader's other inputs were read too: the capture light is −(Rz(45°)·Rx(−50°)·(0,0,1)), fixed to the screen, driving a 32-row table, and the per-channel floors are 0 at capture time — so the viewer's shading is the Saturn's own. The last cosmetic scrap is closed too: POLYHELP.CMP is 43 help messages of 272×16 px 4bpp and POLYBTN.CMP a 256-colour palette plus a 139-sprite atlas, and the six families have no word names — ポリ吉 labels them with the Roman numerals I–VI and calls them ジャンル (genre), reserving “page” for the pages within a genre.

  4. 4

    Bullet steering states 17 / 18 / 19TRACED 2026-09-12

    All three are entries of the 128-entry per-object state table at 0x06084590, and each is state 16 — the plain enemy bullet — plus one timed action, run only while the bullet is alive. The per-bullet fields they steer are traced with them: the distance accumulator, the 8.8 heading, the speed drawn from [128,256,512,896] by the bullet config, and the steer target that geometry 8 alone writes. Also settled on the way: the spawn flag the shooter passes is 1 for a heading-relative volley and 0 for a player-aimed one, which is the opposite of how the name reads. The runtime still flies these straight.

  5. 5

    Bosses and zako are on different hp scalesTUNING

    Both of this item's predecessors are closed, and 2026-09-12 corrected the item itself. Record byte 2 bits 0–2 IS hp, and byte 1's [60,30,15,…] is the animation period — the zako initialiser settles it, and an adversarial pass then confirmed all four field readings from the editor's own widgets as well as the engine. The ladder reads 1/3/5/10/20/40/50/100 full-power hits, and the 256× weapon-damage bug it exposed is fixed.

    Two sentences this item used to carry were wrong. Boss and zako hp do not come off one table at two scales: they are separate tables, 0x06085F20 for zako and 0x06085F40 for bosses, each read only by its own site, with a per-index ratio running from 4000× down to 19.5×. And the “second, non-record caller that shifts the table >>2” is not a boss path at all — it sits inside the fire-point dispatcher, its index is the parent's facing angle masked to three bits rather than any hp field, and for a boss core that angle is always a multiple of 8, so the shifted figure is never computed for a boss. Nothing in the engine produces the 50–488-hit boss the importer's divisor lands on.

    What survives is the number that matters: the record-driven boss core takes its table unshifted through the same difficulty scaler a zako's goes through, so map-to-game.js's ×1024 against a zako's ×256 is a real 4× discount (median ratio exactly 4.0 across every boss record in the corpus). Found alongside it: the scaler has a second multiplier neither FORMAT.md nor the runtime recorded — a further ×1.5 when both players are in play — so every hit figure here is the one-player, normal-difficulty case, and the real envelope runs from 134–1300 to 450–4388. Closing this is a tuning decision, not a trace question.

  6. 6

    The song decoder keyed on the wrong columnFIXED 2026-09-12

    A part's 32-byte step block is a voice column then a pitch column, and decode-song.js decided whether a step sounded by looking at the voice byte — a zero there ended the note. The kernel's note sender does the opposite: it tests the tie bit first, then keys on the pitch byte alone, and the voice byte only selects an instrument. Voice 0 is instrument 0, a real tone-bank voice, not a rest. Over the 258-save corpus that reading dropped 72,282 sounding steps and left 25 songs completely silent — every one of them a song whose composer never moved off instrument 0. The runtime bundle always read the pitch column, so the editor's preview was the half that disagreed with the hardware; the two now match, and 4,311 of the 4,312 non-empty songs in the corpus decode to notes. The one that still does not is a slot whose only pitch bytes are the 231–255 garbage tail, which the decoder clamps away deliberately. Three smaller documentation errors went with it: the per-measure transpose reaches the four pitched accompaniment channels and not the three percussion ones, the sustain byte is only ever 0x80 and never 0x81–0x88, and bit 7 of measure control byte 2 is an undocumented per-measure flag set in most measures.

  7. 7

    Which scale register is the horizontal oneMEANING OPEN

    Opened 2026-09-12, while closing a larger item. The four change channels were re-traced end to end against GAME.bin and four readings turned out to be wrong. Record bytes 6–8 are not a speed channel: they drive 0x06094A40, the third register of the engine's scale triple, which every object initialiser seeds to ×1.0 beside the per-axis pair and which the per-frame pass copies into both hitbox half-extents — a uniform zoom, on 23,552 of the corpus's 75,502 records. The repeat codes 1 and 2 were the wrong way round (1 is ping-pong, 2 is loop; 16,131 records). A ramp is measured by unsigned distance travelled, not by reaching the end value, which is how an angle sweeps the long way round and how a rotation whose start equals its end makes a whole circle (9,664 records). And rotation modes 3 and 4 overwrite the channel's repeat byte with their own number, routing the stepper to a target-tracking arm and killing whatever the record authored (8,490 records).

    That pass also retracted a hardware measurement this page and FORMAT.md had carried since 2026-09-12: that all three scale modes zoom both axes. The spawn routine arms the first register for modes 1 and 2 and the second for modes 1 and 3 — exactly the editor's XY / X / Y labels — and the confound is visible in the very enemy the measurement used, Ramsie's falling rock, which runs a ×1.5→0 one-axis ramp and a ×4→0 uniform zoom at the same time. What is left open is small and cosmetic: the two axis registers are plainly distinct in the trace, but nothing there says outright which of them is the horizontal. The decoder follows the editor's list order; confirming it needs one hardware capture.

  8. 8

    Save-driven endingsNO TRACE

    The ending scene is still stock 2028-AI. Demo/attract recordings live on the retail disc rather than in saves, so this is bounded work — but nothing is decoded for it yet.

02Already implemented

Confirmed Checksum-proven or engine-disassembly-traced, and driven end-to-end by the runtime. These need no further RE work.

Containergzip .bcr / interleaved cart dumps / raw .bkr → BUP partitions, directory entries (Shift-JIS names, dates), fragmented block-chain reassembly, full 0x6C section-table + checksum validation, Okumura LZSS. bup-parse.js · payload-table.js · decompress.js

Enemy AIThe 18-byte record plus the engine's own choreography: all 256 appearance behaviour scripts (1,206 rows) interpreted live, the six hardcoded special classes 0x31–0x36, the 16-entry fire-geometry table with the 10/11/12 burst handlers, the four change channels run the engine's way — a signed accumulator against an unsigned sweep, so an angle can turn the long way round — with their triggers at the traced pixel thresholds, and the hardware terrain-ride model — anchored riders recomputed absolutely from a map anchor, plain riders stepped by the frame's whole pixels, everything else holding station. decode-enemy.js · appearance-table.js · game.bundle.js
Death wordRecord bytes 2–3 — long mis-read as "fire type / fire params" — are the death word, now implemented in full: item drops by authored slot, child spawn of a successor at the dying enemy's exact position (inheriting an anchored parent's terrain anchor), and chain-kill of every object sharing a formation key in a four-tick ripple where each link runs its own death word. Byte 4 selects silent / small / full blast. decode-enemy.js · game.bundle.js
Bosses0x40 trailer decoded (size class, HP bands, pattern playlist, 4×14-byte patterns with 3 fire points) and all 32 engine movement scripts embedded with their step interpreter, including the arrive/death selectors that place the authored off-screen start, the death-drift target and the zoom/spin flourishes. decode-boss.js · data/boss-move-scripts.json
Items & weaponsEight item slots decoded as movement<<4 | type across nine types with their three launch behaviours, drops dressed in the save's own icons; weapon loadout presets and real per-level damage tables replace the refuted "+3 nibble = main weapon" reading. decode-settings.js · map-to-game.js
BGM / SFXsec6 decoded (24 songs × 32 measures × 4 parts × 16 steps, byte-exact tempo table, loop, echo, transpose) and synthesized live in WebAudio from the save's bytes — not prerecorded. Which column starts a note was corrected on 2026-09-12: the pitch column alone, never the voice column (item 6). Timing is engine-exact. Now with the kernel's auto-accompaniment: a measure's control bytes pick seven pattern channels (three chords, a bass, three percussion) out of the 1,260-row ROM table, transposed and voiced exactly as the driver does it. Instruments are the save's own: each note picks its tone-bank voice, and multisampled banks pick the LAYER covering that note. decode-song.js · data/bgm-accompaniment.json · game.bundle.js
TimbresThe 116 tone-bank instruments ship as harmonic profiles derived by spectral analysis of the retail sample bank — analysis output only, never sample audio, which is disc content. Each is rebuilt at runtime as a WebAudio PeriodicWave with the bank's own envelope and level, replacing four fixed square/triangle/noise voices. The instrument map's SNDPAC.BIN offsets and loop points are now read too: hand the runtime the disc's sample bank and it cuts the 85 distinct slices out of it and plays the real Saturn samples, loop points and all — reverse and ping-pong loops baked down to forward loops, every layer of an instrument struck at once so the detune pairs chorus. The bank is disc content, so it is never shipped and never published; without one the periodic-wave voices remain the sound. data/bgm-voices.json · data/bgm-instruments.json · src/audio/tone-bank.js
Player weaponsThe loadout's four presets drive the ship: eight bomb behaviours (flash sphere, rising pillar, tether, expanding ring, lobbed ball, ship aura, ship-as-projectile, rising wave) as piercing per-frame contact objects with their traced growth curves, lifetimes, invincibility and one-in-flight stock rule; the charge gauge with its levels, busy counters and sub-weapon suppression; and the sub weapon at engine-exact cadence. Option pods are real after all — the 2026-08-28 “no option pods” refutation looked at the MAIN field and generalised. The editor's SUB values 5/6/7 are literally named OPTION A/B/C, and they never reach a shot dispatcher: a per-frame hook (+0x1CEDE) drives three handlers that keep a persistent four-slot object block per player. Now implemented (2026-09-02): A is an orbiting ring of up to four indestructible contact-damage pods that widens and speeds up while held, B a flanking pair that aims away from your stick and fires on its own reload, C a trail that rides your path and re-muzzles the MAIN weapon through every pod. The count is the OPTION stat (ship byte +2), which this codebase had been reading as the shot power level. One adaptation: the engine widens A's ring while the C button is held, and since this runtime autofires it has no held-fire button to inherit, so the ring gets its own input exactly as the charge did: Z on a keyboard, M for player two, or either bottom trigger (L2/R2) on a pad — the two buttons that used to be a sixth and seventh way to fire the bomb. The MAIN and SUB labels were transposed and are now fixed (2026-09-02): the editor's own ARMS panel names byte0 bits4-6 MAIN and bits0-2 SUB, so the weapon this runtime implements is the ship's MAIN gun, and its seven values are the editor's VULCAN A, VULCAN B, MISSILE, HOMING, SHADOW, WAVE and BOUND. A charge above 31 suppresses the MAIN weapon always and the SUB only when the SUB's type is 7 — matched here since 2026-09-02, when this runtime stopped suppressing both unconditionally. decode-settings.js · game.bundle.js
Title & staff rollThe save's drawn TITLE 1/2 run the authored entrance program: settings +0x29..+0x2C — the editor's two 5×3 icon grids, the "15 slots" per logo — are ten tri-state fields (vertical entry, horizontal entry, spin, height and width scale per logo), each a straight 128-frame line onto the rest pose, then the 32-frame white flash and the blinking PRESS 1P START BUTTON. FORMAT.md's "one fixed sway-in" was wrong. The ending plays the traced staff roll: the three role labels typed at 4 frames a character over the stages' scenery, each of the six 64×16 credit strips flying in from a corner over 128 ticks (3.0×→1.0×, one turn, afterimage trail, 4× on START), and the title's STAFF ROLL card lists the same labels with their strips. Also found on the way: the BGM table has four special tracks, so every stage's music was one entry off. decode-settings.js · decode-sprites.js · game.bundle.js
Two playersSettings bit1 is live: a second ship joins in mid-run on a free pad (or player 2's own keys), flying the save's own P2 art from global bank refs 24–47 and its own weapon preset from ship block +0x10. Each ship carries its own HP, combo, powerups, barrier, charge, sub weapon and bomb — the engine's one-bomb-per-player rule included — and enemies aim at a single global target that alternates between them on a timer, as traced. One player's death does not end the other's game; the run ends only when both are gone. One deliberate divergence from hardware: score is shared, not per player, and the HUD splits the HP and combo troughs vertically (player 1 on top) rather than showing two rows. decode-sprites.js · map-to-game.js · game.bundle.js
OrientationA horizontal save is the SAME simulation drawn transposed, so the world needed no rotation — what it needed were its own numbers, and it has them: scroll rate ×365/256, a scroll window reaching 96 px further past the entry edge, and the horizontal change-channel trigger lines (14/48/116 px instead of 62/112/164). map-to-game.js · game.bundle.js
3Dsec7 decodes: magic, 16 model slots, 9 parts each, 16.16 positions / rotations / scales with mirror-by-negative-scale, and the shape word's family / colour set / mesh index into the disc's 224-mesh ポリ吉 part library, which ships as mesh-library.json and is drawn by the model viewer on Phaser 4's Mesh2D (CPU projection, painter-sorted, flat-shaded). decode-model.js · decode-mdldt.js · model-mesh.js · model-viewer.html

04No trace at all

Absent Real Dezaemon 2 features with no code behind them.

  • Save-driven endings beyond the staff roll — no ending picture exists on the disc, so what is left is the ranking/name-entry screen (item 8)

Three entries left this list on 2026-09-12. The write path shipped on 2026-09-05 and is item 1 above. What the six ポリ吉 part families are called turned out to be a non-question: the editor numbers them I–VI, and POLYHELP.CMP and POLYBTN.CMP are both decoded (item 3).
